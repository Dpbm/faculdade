Here’s a list of computationally expensive tasks in **bioinformatics**, often requiring high-performance computing (HPC), large memory, or long runtimes due to the complexity or volume of data involved:

-------------------

### 🧬 **1. Genome Assembly (especially de novo assembly)**

* **Why expensive**: Requires aligning and overlapping millions to billions of short/long DNA reads to reconstruct whole genomes without a reference.
* **Tools**: SPAdes, Canu, Flye, HiCanu, Shasta.
* **Bottlenecks**: Memory usage, graph construction, and traversal (e.g., de Bruijn graphs or overlap graphs).

---

### 🧪 **2. Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS) Variant Calling**

* **Why expensive**: Processing large BAM/CRAM files, detecting SNPs, indels, and structural variants from raw reads.
* **Tools**: GATK, DeepVariant, FreeBayes.
* **Bottlenecks**: Read alignment, sorting, and variant calling stages require intensive CPU and I/O.

---

### 🧠 **3. Cryo-Electron Microscopy (Cryo-EM) Image Reconstruction**

* **Why expensive**: Involves 3D reconstruction from thousands to millions of noisy 2D images.
* **Tools**: RELION, cryoSPARC, EMAN2.
* **Bottlenecks**: GPU usage, alignment of large datasets, 3D Fourier transforms.

---

### 🧬 **4. Pan-genome Analysis**

* **Why expensive**: Comparing the genomes of many individuals/species to find conserved and variable regions.
* **Tools**: PanTools, Roary, PGAP.
* **Bottlenecks**: Multiple large-scale alignments and graph-based genome representation.

---

### 🧬 **5. Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA) of Large Datasets**

* **Why expensive**: Aligning hundreds or thousands of long sequences increases time and memory complexity.
* **Tools**: MAFFT, Clustal Omega, MUSCLE.
* **Bottlenecks**: Pairwise comparisons scale quadratically or worse.

---

### 🧬 **6. Phylogenetic Tree Construction (Large Datasets)**

* **Why expensive**: Building accurate evolutionary trees from large aligned sequence datasets.
* **Tools**: RAxML, IQ-TREE, FastTree.
* **Bottlenecks**: Searching huge tree space (NP-hard), bootstrapping for accuracy.

---

### 🧠 **7. Deep Learning on Genomics or Imaging Data**

* **Why expensive**: Training deep neural networks (e.g., CNNs for histopathology, RNNs/Transformers for sequence data).
* **Tools**: TensorFlow, PyTorch, DeepVariant, AlphaFold.
* **Bottlenecks**: GPU/TPU usage, large training datasets, model complexity.

---

### 🧬 **8. Protein Structure Prediction (e.g., with AlphaFold)**

* **Why expensive**: Uses deep learning + massive feature extraction from sequence alignments (MSAs, PSSMs).
* **Tools**: AlphaFold, RoseTTAFold.
* **Bottlenecks**: MSA generation, feature extraction, and inference on large models.

---

### 🔬 **9. Metagenomic Analysis**

* **Why expensive**: Sequencing and analyzing complex microbial communities (billions of reads).
* **Tools**: MetaPhlAn, Kraken2, HUMAnN, MEGAHIT.
* **Bottlenecks**: Taxonomic classification, assembly, binning.

---

### 💾 **10. RNA-Seq Analysis (Bulk and Single-cell)**

* **Why expensive**:

  * **Bulk**: Alignment, quantification, differential expression.
  * **Single-cell**: Demultiplexing, normalization, clustering of high-dimensional data.
* **Tools**: STAR, Salmon, Seurat, Scanpy.
* **Bottlenecks**: Memory and CPU for alignment; GPU for dimensionality reduction in single-cell.

---

### 🧠 **11. Network Biology / Systems Biology Simulations**

* **Why expensive**: Modeling gene regulatory networks or metabolic pathways across time and space.
* **Tools**: CellDesigner, COPASI, BioNetGen.
* **Bottlenecks**: ODE/PDE solving, combinatorial explosion of states.

---

### 🧠 **12. Molecular Dynamics (MD) Simulations**

* **Why expensive**: Simulating atomic-level movements of proteins/DNA over time.
* **Tools**: GROMACS, NAMD, AMBER.
* **Bottlenecks**: Long time scales (microseconds to milliseconds), fine temporal resolution.

---

### 🧬 **13. Large-Scale Data Integration & Annotation (Omics Integration)**

* **Why expensive**: Combining genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics data.
* **Tools**: Galaxy, Bioconductor, OmicsPipe.
* **Bottlenecks**: Data harmonization, storage, heterogeneous formats.
